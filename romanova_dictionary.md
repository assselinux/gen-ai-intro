# A. Romanova Dictionary

## 1. **Hallucination**

When an AI model generates plausible-sounding but completely false or made-up information.

---

## 2. **Fine-tuning**

Taking a pre-trained AI model and training it further on your specific data to make it better at your particular task.

---

## 3. **Prompt Engineering**

The skill of writing clear, specific instructions (prompts) to get better results from an AI model.

---

## 4. **LLM (Large Language Model)**

A massive neural network trained on huge amounts of text data that can understand and generate human language.

---

## 5. **Transformer**

A type of neural network architecture that processes information in parallel and uses "attention" to focus on important parts of data.

---

## 6. **Diffusion Models**

AI models that generate new images or audio by starting with random noise and gradually refining it into clean, coherent output.

---

## 7. **GAN (Generative Adversarial Network)**

Two neural networks competing against each other—one creates fake data, the other tries to detect fakes. This competition improves both.

---

## 8. **VAE (Variational Autoencoder)**

A neural network that learns to compress data into a simpler form and then reconstruct it, creating new variations of similar data.

---

## 9. **Tokenization**

The process of breaking text into smaller pieces (tokens) that the AI model can process. Tokens are usually words or sub-word units.

---

## 10. **Embeddings**

Numbers that represent the meaning of words or images. Similar concepts get similar number patterns.

---

## 11. **Context Window**

The amount of previous conversation or text that the model can "remember" and use when generating a response.

---

## 12. **Inference**

The process of running your trained AI model on new data to get predictions or generate output.

---

## 13. **Data-to-Narrative Transformation**

The process of converting raw numbers and data (like financial statements) into natural language stories and reports.

---

## 14. **Semantic Understanding**

The model's ability to understand the true meaning of words and concepts, not just pattern-matching.

---

## 15. **Synthetic Data**

Artificial data created by AI models (rather than collected from real users) that mimics the patterns of real data.

---

## 16. **Model Accuracy / Evaluation Metrics**

Numerical measurements of how well your model performs on specific tasks (how many correct answers, how realistic the output looks, etc.).

---

## 17. **Computational Overhead / Inference Cost**

The computing power and cost required to run the model and generate output.

---

## 18. **Regulatory Compliance / Bias & Fairness**

Following laws and industry rules when building AI, and ensuring the model doesn't discriminate against certain groups.

---

## 19. **Domain-Specific Fine-tuning**

Training your model specifically on data from your industry or specialty so it learns your unique language, patterns, and rules.

---

## 20. **Real-time Processing / Latency**

The speed at which your model can generate output. Latency is the delay between request and response.
